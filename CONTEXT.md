# Personal Portfolio

A professional portfolio presenting Ivan Siachoque's frontend engineering and
interface work to recruiters evaluating him for employment opportunities.

## Language

**Recruiter**:
A visitor assessing Ivan's professional fit and deciding whether to initiate a
hiring conversation.
_Avoid_: Customer, buyer

**Opening**:
The portfolio's first screen, introducing Ivan, his professional positioning,
and a direct way to take the next step.
_Avoid_: Landing page, splash screen

**Professional headline**:
The public positioning "Senior Frontend Engineer · UI & Motion" used to express
the roles Ivan is targeting, rather than a historical employer's job title.
_Avoid_: Employment title

**Professional chapter**:
A period of Ivan's experience presented with its employer or contractual
relationship, responsibilities, and contributions.
_Avoid_: Project, employer card

**Case study**:
An expanded account of a specific piece of work, distinguishing its context,
Ivan's contributions, and the technical choices supported by available evidence.
_Avoid_: Professional chapter, testimonial

**Conceptual recreation**:
An original visual interpretation of a described work function, explicitly
identified as illustrative rather than an actual client deliverable.
_Avoid_: Client screenshot, production interface

**Conceptual portrait**:
An illustrated representation created for the portfolio's visual identity,
without claiming photographic resemblance to Ivan.
_Avoid_: Photograph, verified likeness
